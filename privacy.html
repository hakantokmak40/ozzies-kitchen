Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

1. Who we are

Ozzie's Kitchen Studio ("the app") is an internal tool operated by Ozzie's Kitchen (the Netherlands). We use it only to prepare and publish Ozzie's Kitchen's own cooking videos to Ozzie's Kitchen's own accounts on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok, and to view how those videos perform. The app is not offered to the public and is not used to access anyone else's account.

2. Data the app accesses

Only after the account owner signs in and grants permission, the app accesses:

  • TikTok: basic profile information (such as display name and avatar), the ability to upload our videos as drafts to our own TikTok inbox, and the list of our own videos with their public statistics (views, likes, comments, shares).
  • Instagram: basic information about our own professional account, the ability to publish our own Reels, and insights (statistics) about our own posts.
  • YouTube: the ability to upload and update videos on our own channel, and analytics about our own channel and videos. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

The app does not collect data about viewers, followers or other users beyond the aggregated statistics that the platforms provide for our own content.

3. How we use the data

Solely to publish Ozzie's Kitchen's own content and to understand which of our videos perform well. We do not sell, rent or share this data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Storage and security

Access tokens and statistics are stored locally on the operator's own computer, not on a public server. Video analysis and text generation run mainly on that computer. If an external AI service is used to help write recipe captions, only our own recipe text is sent to it, never platform account data or tokens.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes above. Access can be revoked at any time:

  • Google/YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions

After access is revoked, we delete the related tokens and stored statistics on request.

6. Contact

Questions or deletion requests: ozzie.kitchen40@gmail.com.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.
